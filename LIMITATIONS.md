# Limitations

VC Answer Map measures outputs from selected models for selected prompts at recorded times.

Key limitations include:

- Model answers are stochastic and can vary between runs.
- API behaviour may differ from consumer products used by founders.
- The eight-family panel does not represent actual founder market share.
- Closed-book prompts cannot prove where knowledge is stored inside a model.
- Search-enabled results depend on provider, location, index and retrieval configuration.
- Prompt wording influences which firms appear.
- Model knowledge can be outdated, incorrect or hallucinated.
- A firm's appearance does not establish suitability, service quality or investment performance.
- Absence from an answer does not establish lack of expertise.
- Self-reported confidence is not calibrated.
- A public benchmark may later influence the systems it measures.

Every published result must include its date, model panel, prompt version, search condition and repetition count.
